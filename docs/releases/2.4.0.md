A volume control on every watched stream, tidier sidebar sections and a stream stage without stray gaps, checked against the live client.

- Streams you watch always have a volume control. TeamSpeak hides its own whenever the streamer's audio setting or its audio flag is off, even though the stream's audio still plays. TS6 Enhanced then adds the same mute button and slider, with 300% and per-streamer memory when those switches are on, and steps aside when TeamSpeak's control returns.
- Clicking around a stream no longer goes fullscreen. A double-click acts as one click (focus or back to the grid); the fullscreen button in the player bar is unchanged.
- The stage meets the channel column at the same inset as its other edges, without the empty arrow rail on the left. Its player bar lines up with the footer line of the side columns.
- TeamSpeak's stream-quality warning sits inside the top-right corner of its player as a framed button, and hides with the preview it belongs to.
- An unopened stream shows who is streaming and the stream's title above the Watch button. Its menu and pop-out buttons appear on hover instead of in a mostly empty bar.
- The chat toggle at the stage's right edge is a full-height strip.
- Sidebar sections share one heading style, Servers included. Add contact and Join group chat are icons in their heading row, shown on hover with the other section tools.
- Offline contacts are dimmed, repeated group badges in the channel tree are quieter until hovered, your own name stands out, and names cut off in the tree show in full on hover.
- The sharing card has equal margins in its column, and a hidden chat no longer adds a scrollbar to the stream stage.

Requires Windows x64 and TeamSpeak 6.0.0-beta5, build 1791901251.

Validation includes the core, browser and native regression suites, plus live inspection of a watched stream with and without TeamSpeak's volume control, the stream stage and the sidebar. The fallback volume control was tested on a live stream whose streamer had audio sharing turned off; streams that really carry no audio show the control too.
