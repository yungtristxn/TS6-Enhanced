Bigger streams in the grid, and zoom into a stream like in Discord.

- **Balanced stream grid**: every stream in the grid now gets the same size, as large as the stage allows. Two streams on a tall stage (for example with chat hidden) are stacked instead of side by side when that makes each one bigger, about 20% larger on a typical 1080p window. Larger stages with three to six streams no longer mix big and small tiles.
- **Scroll to zoom**: scroll over a focused or fullscreen stream, or the only stream on the stage, to zoom in up to 5× on the spot under the pointer. Drag to look around while zoomed. Scroll back out, or click the zoom level in the corner, to reset. Leaving focus or fullscreen resets it too. Zoom can't add detail, so a stream gets soft beyond its own resolution. Pop-out windows don't support it yet.
- Both are on by default and can be switched off on the Streams tab of the TS6 Enhanced dialog.

Requires Windows x64 and TeamSpeak 6. Verified with TeamSpeak 6.0.0-beta5.1.

Validation includes the core, browser and native suites. The grid is checked against the installed client's own layout code, and zoom with real wheel and drag input. Both were also tried in the live client.
