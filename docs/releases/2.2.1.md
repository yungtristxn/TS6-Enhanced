Fixes muted joins after an update restart, stream focus and fullscreen behavior, and visual inconsistencies.

- Fixes joins starting with the microphone and speakers muted after an update restart was interrupted by clicking or typing while TeamSpeak reconnected. The previous mute state is restored, and later joins no longer inherit the temporary mute.
- Reconnected servers get their saved mute state back after an interrupted restore when the identity and channel still match. Saved server tabs keep their own channel settings instead of the restore's temporary ones.
- The stream fullscreen and exit-fullscreen buttons work again. Clicking a focused stream returns to the grid, matching TeamSpeak and the Enter key. Two quick separate clicks no longer enter fullscreen.
- Boosted stream volume above 100% is kept across update restarts for all streams.
- The sharing card no longer covers the last channels in the tree, and the viewer and stream lists close when clicking stream controls.
- Unifies colors and dividers across the sidebar, channel tree and headers, and scales labels and headings with TeamSpeak's Small and Large UI sizes.
- A failed update preparation no longer leaves a saved session behind that could be replayed on the next launch.

Requires Windows x64 and TeamSpeak 6.0.0-beta5, build 1791901251.

Validation includes the core, browser and native regression suites. The update-restart fixes are covered by regression tests; they were not repeated in a live update restart.
