A calmer channel chat, consistent controls, and updates that are faster and can no longer leave TeamSpeak running invisibly.

- The channel chat sits on the message box instead of hanging from the header, and each day starts with a dated line ("Today", "Thursday 29 October"); message times show only the hour. Day separators have their own switch.
- With all streams hidden, a short notice and a **Show streams** button replace TeamSpeak's long sentence that pointed at a menu on the far side of the window.
- The sharing card shows **Live** alone until someone watches, and no longer stretches across the home page.
- Grouped controls share one look: the header's info/chat/files switch and the card's Pause/Settings/Stop match TeamSpeak's toolbar groups, and selected or open controls are distinct from hovered ones. Smaller fixes: a drawn arrow beside the shared screen, popovers as wide as the card, square-cornered "+N more" buttons and a chat strip aligned with the stream.
- Updates restart reliably. A launch that finds a queued update now ends cleanly instead of risking a window-less TeamSpeak that received every later launch until ended in Task Manager. A TeamSpeak stuck while quitting is ended after a minute, and TeamSpeak always reopens once its window has closed. An "Updating TS6 Enhanced" window shows while it is closed.
- Updates are faster: queued updates skip a redundant preparation pass, the 87 MB Node runtime is neither unpacked nor copied when unchanged, and update downloads shrink from about 23 MB to about 2 MB when your runtime already matches (from the next release on). Old rollback copies are pruned, freeing space in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\TS6-Layout`.

Requires Windows x64 and TeamSpeak 6.0.0-beta5, build 1791901251.

Validation includes the core, browser, native and installer suites, with the real update scripts and installer run against isolated stand-in clients (including a client stuck in shutdown and a startup client that never exits), plus live inspection of the chat, stream notices, sharing card and toolbar. The faster installer paths take effect for updates after this one.
